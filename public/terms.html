Using OnceHere

Terms of Use

These terms set basic expectations for using OnceHere. They are written to keep the service useful, safe, and respectful.

Your content and responsibility

You remain responsible for content you submit. Only upload or publish material you have a reasonable right or permission to share. Do not use OnceHere to impersonate someone, expose private information, harass people, or publish unlawful or harmful content.

School, class, and youth archives

Memory archives often include classmates and community members. Creators should use extra care with names, photos, contact details, and stories involving young people. Avoid publishing sensitive personal information and honor reasonable removal requests.

Ownership credentials

The owner recovery key is the credential that proves control of an account-free archive. Keep it private and backed up. Contributor or viewer access does not transfer ownership. Do not share owner credentials with people who should only contribute or view.

Availability and backups

OnceHere is provided on a best-effort basis and may change as the service develops. Creators should keep their own copies of irreplaceable photos and videos. A memory platform should not be the only copy of important media.

Moderation and platform protection

OnceHere may hide, unpublish, restrict, or remove content or archives when reasonably necessary to address abuse, privacy complaints, security risks, legal obligations, or service integrity. Technical limits may also be enforced to protect storage and performance for everyone.

No credential sharing in reports

Never include recovery keys, contributor PINs, viewer PINs, database credentials, or other secrets in reports or support messages.

Changes to the service

Features, storage limits, hosting providers, and these terms may change as OnceHere develops. Material changes should be reflected on this page.
