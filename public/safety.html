Share memories responsibly

Safety & Respect

OnceHere is meant to preserve good memories, not create new problems for the people in them.

Before you publish

  • Use photos and stories you have a reasonable right or permission to share.
  • Ask before posting sensitive or potentially embarrassing material about someone else.
  • Do not publish home addresses, private phone numbers, passwords, school login details, recovery keys, or other sensitive information.
  • For school and youth archives, keep personal details minimal and audience-appropriate.

Choose the right visibility

Use public visibility only for material you are comfortable making broadly accessible. Use unlisted or private visibility when the archive is meant for a smaller group. A PIN reduces access but cannot stop an authorized viewer from taking screenshots or resending content.

Contributors

Give contributor access only to people you trust to add appropriate material. Contributor access is not ownership. Keep the owner recovery key separate and private.

If something should come down

Use the report page for privacy, impersonation, harassment, unwanted photos, or other content concerns. Include the archive link and a clear description of what needs review. Do not include access credentials.

Keep your own backup. OnceHere is a sharing and presentation platform, not a replacement for keeping personal copies of irreplaceable media.
