Content review

Report an archive or item

Use this page for unwanted photos, privacy concerns, impersonation, harassment, harmful content, or other material that should be reviewed.

Never send credentials. Do not include owner recovery keys, contributor PINs, viewer PINs, database credentials, API keys, or passwords in a report.

What to include

  • The OnceHere archive URL.
  • The section or item that needs review.
  • A short explanation of the concern.
  • If you are asking for removal of a photo or profile involving you, enough information to identify the exact item.

Send the report

The current reporting channel is email so a human can review context rather than automatically deleting shared memories.

Urgent danger

If someone may be in immediate physical danger, use appropriate local emergency or trusted-adult support rather than waiting for a website content review.
