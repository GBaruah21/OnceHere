Trust & Privacy

Privacy at OnceHere

OnceHere is built for shared memories. That means photos, names, messages, and other archive content can be personal. This notice explains the platform’s current approach in plain language.

What the platform stores

Archive creators and contributors may add titles, organization or batch details, timeline entries, yearbook profiles, media, and memory-wall posts. Uploaded media is stored in private object storage and is delivered through time-limited signed access paths where appropriate.

Account-free ownership

OnceHere does not require a normal user account for archive ownership. Owners receive a recovery credential and the server stores only its hash. Contributor and private-viewer PINs are also stored as hashes rather than plaintext.

Access and security information

The platform may keep limited technical access information such as session records, timestamps, device type, and an IP hint for security, rate-limiting, and owner access-history features. Do not treat an archive as a place for passwords, financial information, government identifiers, medical records, or other highly sensitive information.

Public, unlisted, and private archives

Creators control an archive’s visibility. Public archives may be discoverable through OnceHere. Unlisted archives are intended to be shared by link. Private archives require the configured viewer access method. Anyone with access to a shared page may still save or reshare what they can see, so only upload content you are comfortable sharing with the intended audience.

Photos and content about other people

Before uploading someone else’s photo, name, message, or other personal content, make sure you have a reasonable right or permission to share it. This is especially important for school and youth archives. Avoid publishing private contact details or embarrassing, harmful, or invasive material.

Removal and correction requests

If you are shown in an archive and want specific content reviewed or removed, use the report page below. Include the archive link and enough detail to identify the content, but do not send passwords or recovery keys.

Data retention

Archive content remains available while the archive exists unless it is removed by an authorized owner or through a platform safety/moderation action. Provider backups and logs may persist for limited operational periods. OnceHere’s infrastructure providers also process data as needed to host the service.

Changes

This notice may be updated as the product and its infrastructure change. The current version shown here applies to the live service.
